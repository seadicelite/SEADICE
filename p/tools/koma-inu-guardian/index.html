Koma-inu Guardian

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described worry or situation to a real regional shrine guardian statue style, explained with the true a-un meaning behind it.

The Problem With This Niche

Koma-inu almost always travel as a photo of two stone lion-dogs at a shrine gate - one mouth open, one closed. Outside Japan, the fact that this pairing is a real documented concept, or that the statues themselves come from distinct regional carving traditions, rarely comes with the picture.

Koma-inu Guardian fills that gap. You describe what's on your mind, and Claude AI, working from documented shrine-guardian history, matches you to one of six real regional carving styles and explains the a-un meaning behind the match.

Key Features

01

One-line description turned into an AI-matched koma-inu style and a-un reading

02

Matched from six real regional styles: Izumo, Naniwa, Okazaki, Edo, Onomichi, Sanuki

03

Real symbolism explained: the a-gyo (open-mouth) and un-gyo (closed-mouth) pairing

04

Each style's regional origin and posture history covered

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
