Mizuhiki Knot

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described occasion to a real mizuhiki cord knot style and explains the etiquette behind it.

The Problem With This Niche

Mizuhiki travels abroad almost entirely as pretty gift string, with little indication that the knot, color, and cord count tied on a Japanese gift envelope are a real etiquette system - and that the wrong choice reads as a genuine social mistake, not a style preference.

Mizuhiki Knot fills that gap. You describe an occasion, and Claude AI, working from documented mizuhiki etiquette, matches it to a real knot style and explains the color and strand-count conventions layered on top of it.

Key Features

01

One-line occasion turned into an AI-matched mizuhiki knot style

02

Matched from three real knots: Musubikiri, Chomusubi, Awaji-musubi

03

Color and strand-count etiquette explained for every match, not just trivia

04

The 607 CE origin story and the Muromachi-period shift to washi cords

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
