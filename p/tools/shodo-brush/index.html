Shodo Brush

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches your mood or goal to a real Japanese calligraphy phrase and brush-stroke style.

The Problem With This Niche

Shodo - Japanese brush calligraphy - is instantly recognizable abroad as an aesthetic, but almost never understood as a practice of choosing the right phrase for the right moment. Outside Japan, there is virtually no App Store product that treats shodo phrases as distinct, real choices suited to different feelings or goals, and no existing app answers the concrete question: "This is what I'm going through right now - which phrase, and which brush style, actually fits?"

Shodo Brush fills that gap. You describe your mood or goal, and Claude AI, in the role of a shodo teacher, matches you to one of eight real phrases, explains why it fits, and gives you the brush-stroke style and origin behind it.

Key Features

01

One-line description of a mood or goal is matched to a phrase by AI

02

Explanation of why the phrase fits, told in the voice of a shodo teacher, plus stroke style and origin note

03

Phrases tab covering all eight real phrases: Ichigo Ichie, Shoshin, Wa, Nintai, Mushin, Heijoshin, Shojin, Kansha

04

Personal history of past matches to revisit later

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
