Kendama Trick Dojo

Describe your skill level and which part keeps missing, and AI matches it to a real named trick, then explains the grip and swing form behind it.

The Problem With This Niche

Most kendama tutorials online jump straight to trick names without connecting them to a beginner's actual sticking point, so people drill the wrong thing and stall for weeks on a trick their grip was never suited for.

Kendama Trick Dojo fills that gap. Describe your skill level and which part keeps missing - the ozara, the kozara, the kensaki, or the moshikame chain - and the AI matches you to a real named trick, its grip, and its swing timing.

Key Features

01

Describe your skill level and sticking point for an AI match to a real named trick

02

Covers moshikame, toudai (lighthouse), hikouki (airplane), and tomeken (spike landing)

03

Explains the specific grip - sagete mochi or a vertical ken hold - each trick depends on

04

Breaks down swing-and-catch timing based on the Japan Kendama Association's own curriculum order

05

Daily free-generation limit with rewarded-ad unlock
