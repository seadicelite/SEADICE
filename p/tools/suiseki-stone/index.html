Suiseki Stone

Describe a found stone's shape and where you found it, and AI matches it to a traditional suiseki viewing-stone classification, then explains the correct display and viewing etiquette.

The Problem With This Niche

Most rock-identification apps tell you what mineral a stone is made of, not what it looks like - and suiseki is entirely about the latter: reading a distant mountain, a waterfall, or an island in a stone's unaltered shape.

Suiseki Stone fills that gap. Describe your stone's shape and where you found it, and the AI classifies it against real suiseki categories and explains the traditional display and viewing etiquette that goes with that classification.

Key Features

01

Describe any found stone's shape and origin to get an AI suiseki classification

02

Matches real traditional forms: distant-mountain (toyama-ishi), waterfall (taki-ishi), island (shima-ishi), and hut (ie-ishi) stones

03

Explains whether a carved daiza stand or a sand-filled suiban tray suits your stone

04

Covers real viewing etiquette, including the stone's "face" (kao) orientation and seasonal display customs

05

Daily free-generation limit with rewarded-ad unlock
