Chochin Glow

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described event or place to the real Japanese chochin (paper lantern) style it belongs to, with shape, region, and calligraphy convention explained.

The Problem With This Niche

Most explanations of chochin treat every lantern as the same generic paper light, skipping the real distinction between yumihari, takahari, goshintou, kanban, and bon-jochin - a distinction that actually determines where and why a given lantern is used.

Chochin Glow fixes that. You describe a scene in a sentence, and Claude AI matches you to the real lantern style, then explains the shape, color, and calligraphy conventions that apply.

Key Features

01

One described scene matched to the real chochin lantern style

02

Covers five genuine categories: yumihari, takahari, goshintou, kanban, bon-jochin

03

Explains real shape, color, and calligraphy conventions

04

Lantern Styles tab for browsing all five categories any time

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
