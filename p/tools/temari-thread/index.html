Temari Thread

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described feeling, occasion, or gift recipient to a real Japanese temari (thread ball) stitch pattern, the kagari technique behind it, and the wish traditionally stitched into that pattern.

The Problem With This Niche

Temari travel almost entirely as a decorative object: a round ball of colorful thread with no explanation of why the pattern looks the way it does. Outside Japan, that image rarely comes with any indication that each geometric design is a named, documented stitch technique, and that the object began as a handmade wish for a child's happiness.

Temari Thread fills that gap. You describe a feeling or occasion, and Claude AI, working from documented temari tradition, matches it to a real stitch pattern and walks you through the kagari technique and the wish behind it.

Key Features

01

One-line description turned into an AI-matched temari stitch pattern

02

Matched from real documented patterns: kiku, asanoha, kikko, matsukawabishi, and more

03

Kagari stitching technique explained step by step for every match

04

Sanuki temari origin story and the regional styles behind the craft

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
