Noren Curtain

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described shop, room, or doorway to a real traditional noren curtain color and fabric, explained with the noren-wake shop-succession tradition.

The Problem With This Niche

A noren almost always travels as a photograph of simple decor: a split fabric curtain in a doorway. Outside Japan, its original job as a color-coded shop sign - and the real reasons behind each color and fabric - rarely comes with the picture.

Noren Curtain fills that gap. You describe a space, and Claude AI, working from documented noren tradition, matches it to one of six real color-and-fabric combinations and explains the shop-sign logic and the noren-wake succession story behind it.

Key Features

01

One-line space description turned into an AI-matched noren color/fabric

02

Matched from six real combinations: indigo, white, tea-brown, purple, crest, seasonal

03

Noren-wake shop-succession story explained for every match

04

Family-crest (kamon) and seasonal natsu-noren variants covered

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
