Ema Wish

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described wish to a real ema wooden wish-plaque tradition and explains the shrine etiquette behind it.

The Problem With This Niche

Ema are one of the most photographed objects at any Japanese shrine, yet most visitors leave knowing only that they're "for wishes" - not that the wish category, the plaque's shape, and even the choice of shrine are supposed to line up with a specific, centuries-old tradition rooted in live horse offerings.

Ema Wish fills that gap. You describe a wish, and Claude AI, working from documented shrine practice, matches it to a real tradition and explains why that shrine and shape fit the wish.
