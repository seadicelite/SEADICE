Sumi-e Ink

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described scene, memory, or feeling to a real sumi-e ink-painting technique and subject, and explains why that technique produces the effect it does.

The Problem With This Niche

Sumi-e travels almost entirely as shorthand for Japanese aesthetics in general - a black circle, a mountain silhouette - with little indication that it is a discipline built around real, named techniques and a hard constraint: once ink touches washi paper, the stroke cannot be corrected or painted over.

Sumi-e Ink fills that gap. You describe a scene or a feeling, and Claude AI, working from documented sumi-e technique and history, matches it to a real technique-and-subject pairing and explains why it produces the visual and emotional effect it does.

Key Features

01

One-line scene or feeling turned into an AI-matched sumi-e technique and subject

02

Matched from four real techniques: Nijimi wet-bleed, Kasure dry-brush, Notan graded tone, Ichigyo-hitsu one-stroke

03

The technique-to-effect relationship explained for every match, not just trivia

04

The ichigo-ichie one-stroke philosophy and the historical schools behind sumi-e

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
