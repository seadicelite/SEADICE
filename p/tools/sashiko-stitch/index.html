Sashiko Stitch

An AI craft app that matches a described mending or decorating project to a real traditional sashiko stitch technique, and explains its regional origin.

The Problem With This Niche

Sashiko tutorials online are usually a single pattern shared without context, or a dense historical essay with no clear starting stitch - leaving a beginner unsure which of several distinct named techniques actually fits their fabric, their skill level, and what they're trying to make.

Sashiko Stitch fills that gap. You describe your project and experience level, and Claude AI, working from documented sashiko technique, matches you to a real stitch method and walks you through the exact sequence to follow.

Key Features

01

One-line project description turned into an AI-matched sashiko technique

02

Real stitch-by-stitch walkthrough with thread, needle, and spacing guidance

03

Genuine moyo-zashi, hitomezashi, and kogin-zashi differences explained

04

Named motif reference: asanoha, shippou, kaki-no-hana, and more

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
