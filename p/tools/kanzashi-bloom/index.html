Kanzashi Bloom

An AI styling app that matches a described occasion, hair length, and kimono or yukata color to a real traditional kanzashi hair ornament style, and explains its seasonal motif.

The Problem With This Niche

Kanzashi guides online are usually a single product photo shared without context, or a dense historical essay with no clear answer for what to actually wear - leaving anyone dressing for a specific occasion unsure which of the many named styles fits their hair, their outfit, and the season.

Kanzashi Bloom fills that gap. You describe your occasion, hair length, and outfit color, and Claude AI, working from documented kanzashi tradition, matches you to a real style and explains the seasonal reasoning and how it is traditionally worn.

Key Features

01

One-line occasion and outfit description turned into an AI-matched kanzashi style

02

Explains the seasonal motif and why it fits your month or occasion

03

Genuine tsumami zaiku, bira-bira, and kushi/kogai differences explained

04

Real guidance on how each style is traditionally positioned and worn

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
