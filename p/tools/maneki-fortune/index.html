Maneki Fortune

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described wish to the real maneki-neko (beckoning cat) paw, color, and pose it belongs to, with Edo-era folklore and shopkeeper etiquette explained.

The Problem With This Niche

Most explanations of maneki-neko treat every figurine as the same generic lucky cat, skipping the real distinction between migi, hidari, kuro, momoiro, and ryote-age - a distinction that actually determines why a given cat sits where it does.

Maneki Fortune fixes that. You describe a wish in a sentence, and Claude AI matches you to the real variant, then explains the paw, color, and etiquette conventions that apply.

Key Features

01

One described wish matched to the real maneki-neko paw, color, and pose

02

Covers five genuine categories: migi, hidari, kuro, momoiro, ryote-age

03

Explains the Gotokuji temple origin legend and real etiquette conventions

04

Fortune Types tab for browsing all five categories any time

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
