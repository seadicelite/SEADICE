Torii Gate Guide

Describe the deity, occasion, and region of a shrine visit, and AI matches it to a real torii gate style, then explains the correct passing-through etiquette.

The Problem With This Niche

Most travel apps treat a torii gate as a photo backdrop rather than a structure with real regional and religious meaning - every gate marks the same threshold, but the structural family behind it (Shinmei, Myojin, Kasuga, Inari) tells a different story.

Torii Gate Guide fills that gap. Describe your shrine visit's context, and the AI matches it against real torii classifications and explains the structural cues and etiquette that go with that style.

Key Features

01

Describe a shrine's deity, occasion, and region to get an AI torii-style match

02

Covers real structural families: Shinmei, Myojin, Kasuga, and Inari senbon-torii

03

Explains structural cues like crossbeam curve and the gakuzuka plaque

04

Walks through real passing-through etiquette, including why the center path is left open

05

Daily free-generation limit with rewarded-ad unlock
