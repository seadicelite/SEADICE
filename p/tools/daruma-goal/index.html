Daruma Goal

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described personal goal to a real traditional Daruma-doll color and the left-eye/right-eye goal ritual, explained with the Takasaki Daruma origin story.

The Problem With This Niche

Daruma dolls travel almost entirely as a souvenir photograph: a round, red, faceless figure with two blank eyes. Outside Japan, that image rarely comes with any explanation of the actual goal-setting ritual it was made for, or why the eyes are left blank in the first place.

Daruma Goal fills that gap. You describe a goal, and Claude AI, working from documented Daruma tradition, matches it to a real traditional color and walks you through painting the left eye when you commit and the right eye only once the goal is achieved.

Key Features

01

One-line goal description turned into an AI-matched Daruma color

02

Matched from real traditional colors: red, gold, green, purple, and more

03

Left-eye/right-eye ritual explained step by step for every match

04

Takasaki Daruma origin story and the Daruma Kuyo year-end ritual

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
