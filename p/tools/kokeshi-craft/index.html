Kokeshi Craft

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a personality or a gift recipient to a real traditional Japanese kokeshi doll lineage.

The Problem With This Niche

Kokeshi dolls are one of the most recognizable Japanese folk crafts abroad, but they are almost always sold as an undifferentiated souvenir. Outside Japan, there is virtually no product that treats kokeshi as what it actually is: a family of at least eleven distinct regional lineages, each from a specific Tohoku hot-spring town, with its own head shape, painting pattern, and craft history.

Kokeshi Craft fills that gap. You describe a personality, and Claude AI, in the role of a kokeshi artisan, matches it to one of eight real lineages, explains why it fits, and gives you the region and craft history behind it.

Key Features

01

One-line personality description is matched to a lineage by AI

02

Explanation of why the lineage fits, plus region origin and painting motifs

03

Lineages tab covering all eight real styles: Naruko, Tsuchiyu, Togatta, Yajiro, Sakunami, Zao Takayu, Nanbu, Tsugaru

04

Personal history of past matches to revisit later

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
