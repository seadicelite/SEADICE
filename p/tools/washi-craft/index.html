Washi Craft

An AI matching app that matches a described gift occasion and mood to a real traditional washi paper type, and explains its regional origin and fiber source.

The Problem With This Niche

Washi guides online are usually a single product photo shared without context, or a dense craft-history essay with no clear answer for what to actually buy - leaving anyone shopping for a specific gift unsure which of the many named varieties fits their occasion and mood.

Washi Craft fills that gap. You describe your gift occasion and mood, and Claude AI, working from documented washi tradition, matches you to a real paper type and explains its regional origin, fiber source, and traditional use.

Key Features

01

One-line occasion and mood description turned into an AI-matched washi paper type

02

Explains regional origin (Mino, Echizen, and more) and traditional fiber source

03

Genuine chiyogami, tengujo, and regional variety differences explained

04

Real guidance on how each washi type is traditionally purchased and used

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
