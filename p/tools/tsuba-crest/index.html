Tsuba Crest

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a description of yourself to a real historical samurai sword-guard design school and motif, explained with the documented craft tradition behind it.

The Problem With This Niche

A tsuba almost always travels as a photograph of decoration: a round metal guard, sometimes framed on a wall. Outside Japan, the fact that its school, metal, and motif once signaled the wearer's rank and temperament rarely comes with the picture.

Tsuba Crest fills that gap. You describe yourself, and Claude AI, working from documented sword-fitting history, matches you to one of six real design schools and explains the craft tradition and symbolism behind the match.

Key Features

01

One-line self-description turned into an AI-matched tsuba school and motif

02

Matched from six real schools: Higo, Owari, Umetada, Nara, Shoami, Goto

03

Real metalwork techniques explained: sukashi, zogan inlay, nara-bori relief

04

Each school's historical patronage and craft lineage covered

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
