Hina Doll

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described space, budget, and taste to the real tier of Hina Matsuri doll display that fits, and explains what every figure means.

The Problem With This Niche

Hina dolls almost always travel abroad as one generic "cute doll" image, stripped of the real tiers, named court figures, and regional conventions that make the tradition what it is. First-time buyers frequently end up with a display that doesn't fit their room or budget, simply because no single tier was ever explained.

Hina Doll fills that gap. You describe your space, budget, and taste, and Claude AI, working from documented Hina Matsuri tradition, matches you to a real display tier and explains every figure in it.

Key Features

01

One-line description turned into an AI-matched Hina display tier

02

Matched from real tiers: Shinno-kazari, Sandan-kazari, Godan-kazari, Nanadan-kazari

03

Every figure explained by name and role, from Odairi-sama to the Gonin-bayashi musicians

04

Real Kyoto-style vs Kanto-style seating-order difference included in each match

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
