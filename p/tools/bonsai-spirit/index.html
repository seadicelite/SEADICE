Bonsai Spirit

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described personality or living space to a real bonsai training style and tree species, explained with wiring and pruning fundamentals.

The Problem With This Niche

Bonsai travels almost entirely as a single finished photograph. Outside Japan, that image rarely comes with any explanation of which of the classic training styles it belongs to, what species it is, or how the shape was actually built.

Bonsai Spirit fills that gap. You describe a personality, a mood, or a space, and Claude AI, working from documented bonsai tradition, matches you to the style and species that fit and explains the wiring and pruning behind the shape.

Key Features

01

One-line personality or space description turned into an AI-matched style

02

Matched from five real training styles: Chokkan, Moyogi, Shakan, Kengai, Ishitsuki

03

Wiring and pruning fundamentals included with every match

04

Styles tab covering all five training forms as a standalone reference

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
