Furin Chime

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described mood, memory, or season to a real Japanese furin (wind chime) material and regional craft, and explains why that material produces the sound it does.

The Problem With This Niche

Furin travel almost entirely as an undifferentiated summer souvenir: a small bell that tinkles in the wind, with no indication that "furin" covers several distinct crafts. Outside Japan, that image rarely comes with any sense that the material - glass, cast iron, forged iron, or brass - is the entire reason each furin sounds different, or that each material comes from its own named regional workshop tradition.

Furin Chime fills that gap. You describe a mood or memory, and Claude AI, working from documented furin craft tradition, matches it to a real material and craft and explains the physical reason it sounds the way it does.

Key Features

01

One-line mood or memory turned into an AI-matched furin material and craft

02

Matched from four real furin traditions: Edo glass, Nanbu Tekki iron, Myochin Hibashi, Takaoka brass

03

The tone-to-material relationship explained for every match, not just trivia

04

The summer custom of hanging a furin, and where each craft comes from

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
