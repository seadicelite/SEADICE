Ukiyo-e Vision

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described scene or feeling to a real ukiyo-e woodblock print, explained with the actual printmaking technique behind it.

The Problem With This Niche

Ukiyo-e prints - the Great Wave, Hiroshige's rain-swept bridges, Utamaro's portraits - are among the most reproduced images from Japan, printed on everything from mugs to phone cases. Almost none of that reproduction carries any of the actual context: who made the print, what it depicts, or how a design was physically separated into keyblocks and hand-brushed color blocks to produce it.

Ukiyo-e Vision fills that gap. You describe a scene or feeling, and Claude AI, working from a curated set of real documented prints, matches you to the one that fits and explains both the artwork and the woodblock process behind it.
