Japan's paperwork is confusing even for locals

City hall forms, residence card rules, national health insurance, garbage sorting calendars, bank account requirements, phone contracts — each comes with its own process, and almost none of it is explained in English. Searching in a hurry often turns up outdated or conflicting information. Japan Life Guide gives you a plain-English starting point: describe your situation, get a clear step-by-step plan, and read pre-written guides on the procedures every newcomer needs first.
