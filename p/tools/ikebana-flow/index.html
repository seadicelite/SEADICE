Ikebana Flow

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a described feeling or space to a real ikebana school and arrangement style, explained with the structure behind it.

The Problem With This Niche

Ikebana travels almost entirely as a single beautiful photo. Outside Japan, that image rarely comes with any of the reasoning behind it - which of the three major schools it belongs to, whether it's a shallow moribana or a tall nageire, or why the stems sit at the angles they do.

Ikebana Flow fills that gap. You describe a feeling, an occasion, or a space, and Claude AI, working from a curated set of real documented arrangements, matches you to the school and style that fits and explains the structure behind it.

Key Features

01

One-line feeling or space description turned into an AI-matched school and style

02

Matched from real Ikenobo, Sogetsu, and Ohara arrangement styles

03

Shin-soe-tai structure breakdown included with every match

04

Schools tab covering rikka, shoka, moribana, and nageire

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
