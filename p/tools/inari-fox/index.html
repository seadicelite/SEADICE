The Gap This Fills

Inari shrines are the most common type of Shinto shrine in Japan, with over 30,000 across the country, but most visitors only know Fushimi Inari's torii-tunnel photo spot and have no way to connect their own wish - business, harvest, protection, travel - to the shrine style and fox symbolism actually meant for it.

Inari Fox Guide closes that gap: describe what you're hoping for, and Claude AI matches you to a real dedication style, explains what the fox messenger's key, jewel, rice sheaf, or scroll represents, and walks through correct offering etiquette.
