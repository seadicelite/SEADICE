Chindogu Lab

An AI invention app that turns a described daily annoyance into a whimsical, deliberately un-useless Chindogu, explaining which of the Ten Tenets of Chindogu it satisfies.

The Problem With This Niche

Most "invention idea generator" tools either sound like earnest startup pitches or produce random noun-mashup jokes with no coherent logic behind them - neither captures what makes Kenji Kawakami's real Chindogu movement funny: a strict, documented rulebook that every invention has to satisfy.

Chindogu Lab fills that gap. You describe a real daily annoyance, and Claude AI, working from Kawakami's actual Ten Tenets of Chindogu, invents a specific device and explains exactly which tenets it satisfies and why it could never be sold.

Key Features

01

Describe any daily annoyance and get an AI-invented Chindogu to (almost) solve it

02

Each invention explains which of the Ten Tenets of Chindogu it satisfies and why

03

Grounded in Kenji Kawakami's real 1980s-90s Chindogu philosophy, not generic gadget humor

04

Tenet Library tab for reading all ten tenets without generating anything

05

Daily free-generation limit with rewarded-ad unlock
