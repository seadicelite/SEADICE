Onsen Manner

An AI travel app for overseas visitors that matches a described trip to a real type of Japanese hot spring, and explains genuine bathing etiquette.

The Problem With This Niche

Onsen etiquette rarely reaches first-time visitors before they're already at the entrance, and the advice that does circulate online is often vague or outdated - especially on tattoo policy, which varies enormously between establishments and is one of the most common sources of confusion and anxiety for visitors.

Onsen Manner fills that gap. You describe your trip and preferences, and Claude AI, working from documented onsen custom, matches you to a real bathing format and walks you through the exact etiquette sequence to follow.

Key Features

01

One-line trip description turned into an AI-matched onsen bathing type

02

Real etiquette walkthrough: washing before entering, no swimsuits, towel handling

03

Genuine rotenburo, uchiyu, ashiyu, and kashikiri-buro differences explained

04

Tattoo-friendly bathing guidance covering private and family bath options

05

Daily free-match limit with rewarded-ad unlock
