Goshuin Quest

An AI lifestyle app for overseas users that matches a shrine or temple visit to a real goshuin seal-stamp tradition and explains the stamp book etiquette behind it.

The Problem With This Niche

Goshuin pages are among the most photographed and shared souvenirs of a Japan trip, yet most visitors never learn what the seal design or calligraphy actually says, why the stamp costs money, or why casually asking for one at the main hall (rather than the dedicated stamp office) is considered poor etiquette.

Goshuin Quest fills that gap. You describe a shrine or temple, and Claude AI, working from documented practice, matches it to the real goshuin tradition and etiquette tied to that place.
