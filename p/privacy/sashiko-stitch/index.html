SEADICE ("we", "us", "our") sets out this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of personal information in the iOS app "Sashiko Stitch" (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide (Stored On-Device)

The App uses the free-text project description that you enter - such as what you're mending or decorating and your stitching experience level - to generate a sashiko technique match and its stitch walkthrough. This information is stored, together with the generated match, only on your device (via local app storage) and is not permanently stored on our servers.

  • The project description text you enter, including the item, fabric, and your skill level
  • The AI-generated match result (sashiko technique and stitch guidance) and your match history

Sending Data to AI

To provide the matching feature, the App sends the project description you enter to Anthropic's Claude API, which generates the match. The submitted content is used only to process that match request and is not permanently stored on our servers. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy for how they handle data sent to their API.

https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Automatically Collected Information

The App uses Google AdMob's advertising SDK, which may collect the following information needed to deliver ads.

  • Device type, OS version, and language settings
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • App usage data (ad impressions and taps)
  • Approximate location (estimated from IP address)

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's features (sashiko technique matching, Pattern Library reference tab, match history, etc.)
  • To deliver targeted advertising through AdMob
  • To investigate and fix bugs and improve the App

3. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases.

  • Sending your input to Anthropic (Claude API) to provide the AI matching feature
  • With your consent
  • Where required by law

4. Third-Party Services

Anthropic (Claude API)

The App uses Anthropic's Claude API for its AI matching feature. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy at the URL below.

https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to deliver ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy at the URL below.

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Your input and match history are stored only on your device. To delete this data:

  • Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device

To request deletion of data collected by AdMob, please contact Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. About the Generated Matches

The sashiko technique matches and stitch guidance provided by this App are educational and reference content based on documented sashiko craft tradition. They are not a substitute for hands-on instruction, and are not intended as expert advice. Always work on a scrap of fabric first when trying a new technique.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. Users under 13 should use the App only with a parent or guardian's consent.
