SEADICE ("we", "us", "our") sets out this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of personal information in the iOS app "Chindogu Lab" (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide (Stored On-Device)

The App uses the free-text description that you enter - such as the everyday annoyance you want addressed - to generate an invented Chindogu concept and its tenet explanation. This information is stored, together with the generated result, only on your device (via local app storage) and is not permanently stored on our servers.

  • The annoyance description text you enter
  • The AI-generated invention result (Chindogu concept and tenet explanation) and your saved gallery

Sending Data to AI

To provide the invention-generation feature, the App sends the description you enter to Anthropic's Claude API, which generates the result. The submitted content is used only to process that request and is not permanently stored on our servers. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy for how they handle data sent to their API.

https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Automatically Collected Information

The App uses Google AdMob's advertising SDK, which may collect the following information needed to deliver ads.

  • Device type, OS version, and language settings
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • App usage data (ad impressions and taps)
  • Approximate location (estimated from IP address)

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's features (Chindogu generation, Tenet Library reference tab, saved gallery, etc.)
  • To deliver targeted advertising through AdMob
  • To investigate and fix bugs and improve the App

3. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases.

  • Sending your input to Anthropic (Claude API) to provide the AI invention-generation feature
  • With your consent
  • Where required by law

4. Third-Party Services

Anthropic (Claude API)

The App uses Anthropic's Claude API for its AI invention-generation feature. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy at the URL below.

https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to deliver ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy at the URL below.

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Your input and saved gallery are stored only on your device. To delete this data:

  • Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device

To request deletion of data collected by AdMob, please contact Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

The Chindogu inventions and tenet explanations provided by this App are fictional, humorous creative content generated for entertainment purposes. They are not real product designs, are not intended to be built, patented, manufactured, or sold, and are not a substitute for professional product-design or legal advice.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. Users under 13 should use the App only with a parent or guardian's consent.
