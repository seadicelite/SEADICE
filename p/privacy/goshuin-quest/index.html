Goshuin Quest

Developer: SEADICE  |  Last updated: August 14, 2026

To generate your match, this app sends the shrine or temple text you enter to Anthropic's Claude API. Your input and the resulting match are stored only on your device and are not permanently stored on our servers.

SEADICE ("we", "us", "our") sets out this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of personal information in the iOS app "Goshuin Quest" (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide (Stored On-Device)

The App uses the free-text shrine or temple description that you enter to generate a goshuin tradition match and explanation. This information is stored, together with the generated match, only on your device (via local app storage) and is not permanently stored on our servers.

  • The shrine or temple text you enter
  • The AI-generated match result (tradition, calligraphy, seal imagery, explanation) and your match history
  • Notes and entries saved to the Journey Log

Sending Data to AI

To provide the matching feature, the App sends the shrine or temple text you enter to Anthropic's Claude API, which generates the match. The submitted content is used only to process that match request and is not permanently stored on our servers. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy for how they handle data sent to their API.

https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Automatically Collected Information

The App uses Google AdMob's advertising SDK, which may collect the following information needed to deliver ads.

  • Device type, OS version, and language settings
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • App usage data (ad impressions and taps)
  • Approximate location (estimated from IP address)

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's features (goshuin matching, Journey Log storage, etc.)
  • To deliver targeted advertising through AdMob
  • To investigate and fix bugs and improve the App

3. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases.

  • Sending your input to Anthropic (Claude API) to provide the AI matching feature
  • With your consent
  • Where required by law

4. Third-Party Services

Anthropic (Claude API)

The App uses Anthropic's Claude API for its AI matching feature. Please refer to Anthropic's Privacy Policy at the URL below.

https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to deliver ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy at the URL below.

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Your input, match history, and Journey Log entries are stored only on your device. To delete this data:

  • Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device

To request deletion of data collected by AdMob, please contact Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. About the Generated Matches

The tradition and etiquette matches provided by this App are educational and reference content based on documented goshuin custom and shrine/temple practice. They are not a substitute for guidance from shrine or temple staff or a professional cultural consultant, and are not intended as religious instruction. Historical and cultural details are presented as general reference and simplified for a casual audience.

7. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. Users under 13 should use the App only with a parent or guardian's consent.

We may revise this policy as needed. Material changes will be announced within the App or on this page.
