Promotional Text
Ever wondered what your name would look like as a real Japanese seal? Enter your name and let an AI hanko carver choose the kanji, script, and blessing for a seal that's uniquely yours.
Description
Long before signatures, Japan sealed its contracts, art, and identity with carved stamps called hanko. Hanko Spirit brings this centuries-old craft to your pocket: enter your name, and optionally what you'd like the seal to represent, and our AI hanko carver gets to work. In moments, you'll receive one to three kanji characters chosen for how they sound and what they mean, their romaji reading, and a short atmospheric "seal reading" written in the voice of a traditional craftsman — explaining the symbolism behind the kanji and recommending whether your seal suits use as a jitsuin (registered legal seal), ginkoin (bank seal), or mitomein (everyday seal), the three real categories every hanko in Japan belongs to. Each carving is rendered as a circular seal certificate, echoing the look of an actual stamped hanko impression, and saved to your history so you can look back on every name and intention you've explored. The Codex tab is a genuine reference guide to Japanese seal culture: registered seals, bank seals, everyday seals, an artist's pen-name seal, the ancient seal script called tensho, the red ink called shuniku, and the seal-registration process that makes a hanko legally binding — all explained in an easy accordion format. A limited number of free carvings are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料彫刻回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された名前・願いごとのテキストは診断結果を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は診断リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

欧米には署名文化はあっても、実印・銀行印・認印を使い分ける印章文化そのものが存在しません。Hanko Spiritは、誰もが持つ「自分の名前」というパーソナルな情報を、日本の印章職人の視点で漢字・書体・意味へと変換することで、単なる文化紹介ではなく「自分だけの一点物」を持ち帰れる体験に変えます。
