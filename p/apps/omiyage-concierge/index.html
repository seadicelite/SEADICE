Promotional Text
Never wonder what omiyage to bring again. Get AI-picked Japanese gift ideas by region, recipient, and budget — plus the etiquette and phrase to say when giving it.
Description
Omiyage — the Japanese custom of bringing back a thoughtful gift whenever you travel, visit someone, or return from a trip — is one of Japan's most quietly demanding social rituals, and one that trips up even experienced travelers and expats. Which regional specialty should you pick? How much should you spend for a boss versus a close friend? How should it be wrapped and handed over? Omiyage Concierge turns that uncertainty into a simple conversation. Tell the app which region your gift is from, who it's for, the occasion, and your budget, and Claude AI instantly suggests three well-matched omiyage categories — never a specific brand or store, just genuine cultural guidance — along with a concrete etiquette tip on presentation and a short Japanese phrase, written in romaji with an English translation, to say the moment you hand the gift over. A separate Meibutsu Atlas tab is a standalone reference guide to ten of Japan's most famous regional specialties, from Hokkaido's milk-and-butter sweets to Kanazawa's delicate gold-leaf confections, each explained with the local history and geography behind it — useful even without asking the AI a single question. Every suggestion is saved to your personal history so you can revisit past gift decisions before your next trip or visit. A limited number of free AI suggestions are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料AI提案回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された地域・相手・シーン・予算・任意メモは提案を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は提案リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

「おみやげ」は日本文化の中でも特に体系立って説明されることが少ない社会儀礼であり、海外の旅行者や在日外国人にとっては「何を」「誰に」「いくらで」「どう渡すか」の全てが暗黙知として存在しています。Omiyage Conciergeは、この暗黙知をAIコンシェルジュとして即座に言語化することで、単なる文化紹介コンテンツではなく、旅行や出張のたびに実際に使われる実用ツールとしての価値を提供します。
