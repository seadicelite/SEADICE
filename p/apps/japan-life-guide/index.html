Description

Just arrived in Japan and not sure where to start? Japan Life Guide explains everyday procedures in plain, easy English — no Japanese required.

Type what you're dealing with, like "I need to register my address at city hall" or "how do I sort garbage in my area," and the AI breaks it down into clear, simple steps. A built-in library also covers the procedures nearly every newcomer needs first, so you can read them anytime without waiting on AI.

■ Who it's for
- Foreign workers who just started a job in Japan
- International students settling into a new city
- Family members joining someone already living in Japan
- Anyone who finds Japanese government paperwork confusing in English

■ Key features
- AI situation guide: describe your situation and get a step-by-step plan in plain English
- Procedure library: pre-written guides on city hall registration, residence cards, national health insurance, garbage rules, bank accounts, and phone contracts
- Guidance history: past AI answers are saved so you can revisit them anytime
- Clear limits stated on every answer: this is general guidance, not legal or immigration advice
- Simple, calm design built for a stressful first few weeks in a new country

This app provides general, plain-English guidance only. It is not legal, immigration, or tax advice. Always confirm final details with your local city hall, the Immigration Services Agency, your employer, school, or a licensed professional.

Free AI guidance has a daily limit (see the app for details). No ads are shown.

About ads

Japan Life Guide shows no advertising of any kind — no banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads. Instead, AI guidance has a daily free-use limit. Once the limit is reached, the app simply shows "You've reached today's free limit. Please try again tomorrow." There is no ad-based way to unlock more.

Why this app exists

Japan's labor shortage is bringing more foreign workers and international students than ever, but the everyday paperwork of settling in — city hall registration, residence cards, health insurance, garbage rules, banking, phone contracts — is still mostly explained in Japanese. Outdated forum posts and machine-translated government pages leave newcomers guessing at exactly the moment they need clear answers. Japan Life Guide gives anyone starting a new life in Japan a plain-English, step-by-step starting point, without the cost or wait of a translator for every small question.
