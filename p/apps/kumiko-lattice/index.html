Promotional Text
Share a short reflection on your day, and let an AI kumiko craftsman match it to a traditional Japanese wood-joinery lattice pattern - assembled from hand-cut pieces with no nails - and its centuries-old meaning.
Description
Kumiko is the quiet Japanese craft behind the geometric wooden lattices found in shoji screens and temple transoms - patterns built from dozens of small, precisely hand-cut pieces of wood, fitted together with no nails and no glue at the joints. Each pattern carries its own traditional meaning, passed down through generations of craftsmen. Kumiko Lattice brings this centuries-old art to your pocket as a moment of daily reflection. Simply share a short thought about your day, your feelings, or a decision ahead of you, and our AI - guided by the spirit of a kumiko workshop master - selects the single pattern from ten traditional designs (including Asanoha, Kikkou, Shippou, and Sayagata) that best resonates with what you shared, and explains why in a warm, contemplative reading under 110 words. Every reading connects your reflection to the pattern's traditional symbolism - growth, longevity, protection, unbroken lineage - and to the quiet discipline of the craft itself: patience, precision, and many small pieces joined into one whole. Browse the full Encyclopedia tab to learn all ten patterns and their meanings at any time, and revisit past readings in your History. A limited number of free readings are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料リーディング回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された気持ち・状況のテキストはリーディング結果を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は当該リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

海外では「ikigai」や「wabi-sabi」のような日本発の概念コンテンツはすでに人気がありますが、それに続く新しい日本文化の切り口はまだ少ないのが現状です。Kumiko Latticeは、建築・インテリア好きの海外ユーザーからひそかに注目される「組子」という職人技を、誰もが日々抱える「今の気持ち」というパーソナルな入り口から体験できる形に変え、単なる工芸紹介ではなく「自分ごと」として楽しめるリーディング体験に仕立てています。
