Description
Washi is not simply "Japanese paper" - it is a living craft tradition where the fiber source, the region, and the papermaking technique itself carry centuries of specific meaning. Mino washi from Gifu, prized for its strength and historically used for shoji screens and account books; Echizen washi from Fukui, one of Japan's oldest paper-producing regions, closely tied to fine art and bank-note-grade paper; chiyogami, woodblock-printed patterned washi used for gift wrapping and origami; and tengujo, an extraordinarily thin yet strong washi historically used for typing carbon paper and now prized internationally for art restoration and bookbinding. Washi Craft turns "what washi should I choose" into a real, matched answer. Describe your gift occasion - a wedding congratulatory envelope, a New Year greeting card, a keepsake box lining, a piece of framed art, or a hand-folded origami gift - along with the mood you want, and our AI matches you to one of several real traditional washi types, explaining the paper's regional origin, its fiber (kozo, mitsumata, or gampi), and its traditional and practical uses. Every match explains why that washi variety fits your occasion and mood in plain language, plus a short note on how it is traditionally purchased or worked with - sheet size conventions, whether it needs backing, and common tools. The Paper Library tab lets you browse every washi type and region as a standalone reference at any time, even without running a match.

Why This App

Washi guides online are usually a single product photo shared without context, or a dense craft-history essay with no clear answer for what to actually buy or use for a specific gift, leaving anyone shopping for a particular occasion unsure which of the many named varieties even applies. Washi Craft starts from something anyone can describe in a sentence - the occasion and the mood - and turns it into a real matched paper type plus the regional reasoning and usage guidance behind it, so a tradition that can feel opaque from the outside becomes an approachable, explained choice.
