概要
Japan's festival calendar is packed with unforgettable events — but knowing which one actually fits your trip is hard to figure out from the outside. Should you time your visit around fireworks and street food, a river of lantern-lit boats, or a mountain town's towering wooden floats? Static travel guides list festivals one by one; they don't tell you which one is right for you. Matsuri Compass asks two simple things: when you're visiting Japan, and what you're hoping to experience, in your own words. Claude AI, acting as a knowledgeable festival guide, matches you to the single best-fitting matsuri from ten of Japan's most iconic festivals, spanning every season — from Sapporo's February snow sculptures to Kyoto's month-long Gion Matsuri and Tokushima's August dance festival, Awa Odori. Every match comes with a short explanation of why it fits, one etiquette or custom tip a first-time foreign visitor should know, and one practical tip for actually seeing it well. A separate Almanac tab lets you swipe through all ten festivals month by month any time, even before you've asked a question — perfect for browsing while you're still deciding on travel dates. Every match you receive is saved to your personal history, so you can compare festivals as your plans firm up. Whether you're building an itinerary from scratch, choosing between two trip windows, or simply curious what's happening in Japan during a particular month, Matsuri Compass turns "which festival should I see?" into a clear, personal answer.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告に加え、1日の無料マッチ回数（5回）を超えた場合はリワード広告の視聴で追加マッチ（+3回）が可能です。マッチング機能のためユーザーが選択した月と入力した「体験したいこと」のテキストのみをAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信しますが、氏名・連絡先等を特定できる情報は送信しません。

今後実装予定の機能

  • 祭りの開催地を地図上で確認できる機能
  • マッチ結果をSNSでシェアする機能
  • フランス語・スペイン語など対応言語の追加

なぜこのアプリが必要か

日本の祭りは数も種類も豊富ですが、それぞれ開催時期・雰囲気・作法が大きく異なり、海外の旅行者が自分の旅程に合う一つを選ぶのは簡単ではありません。単なる祭り一覧の羅列にとどまらず、旅行時期と興味に寄り添っておすすめを提示するAIガイドがいることで、「せっかく日本に行くなら、自分に合った祭りを見逃したくない」という不安を安心に変え、旅の計画をより楽しいものにします。
