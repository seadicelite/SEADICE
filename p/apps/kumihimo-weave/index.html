Promotional Text
Describe a mood, occasion, or intention, and let AI match it to one of eight real, historically documented kumihimo braid patterns - the silk-cord craft once used for samurai armor and kimono sashes.
Description
Kumihimo is the centuries-old Japanese craft of braiding silk cords by hand - once used to lace samurai armor, wrap sword hilts, tie Buddhist prayer beads, and cinch kimono sashes. Each named pattern carries its own history, and Kumihimo Weave brings that hidden world to your pocket. Simply describe what's on your mind - a mood, an upcoming event, a feeling you can't quite name - and Claude AI, acting as a master kumihimo artisan, matches your words to the single best-fitting pattern from a roster of eight real braid types: Kongou Gumi's battle-tested strength, Kikko Gumi's wish for longevity, Karakumi's quiet spiritual protection, and five more, each explained in a warm, craftsman's voice along with a pairing of two traditional Japanese thread colors and the reasoning behind them. Every reading is saved to your personal history so you can look back on past matches, and the Patterns tab holds a full encyclopedia of all eight braids with their kanji and background - useful both as a keepsake and as a genuine primer before shopping for kimono accessories or trying the craft yourself. A limited number of free readings are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料診断回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された気分・出来事のテキストは診断結果を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は診断リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

海外では「ikigai」や「wabi-sabi」のような日本発の概念コンテンツはすでに人気がありますが、組紐のように「物としては見慣れているが、その背景にある本格的な工芸文化までは知られていない」ニッチはまだ多く残されています。Kumihimo Weaveは、誰もが持つ「今の気分」というパーソナルな入力を通じて、実在する伝統工芸の世界を「自分ごと」として楽しめる体験に変えます。
