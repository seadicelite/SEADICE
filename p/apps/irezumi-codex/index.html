Description
Irezumi is Japan's centuries-old art of traditional tattooing, built on a rich vocabulary of creatures, deities, and flowers, each carrying its own layered meaning. Koi represent perseverance, dragons embody wisdom and protection, tigers ward off misfortune, and the hannya mask captures the transformation of rage into hard-won wisdom. Most people encounter these images as striking designs without ever learning what they actually mean. Irezumi Codex changes that. Simply describe a quality, story, or turning point in your life that you want to honor or commemorate, and our AI studies your words alongside a curated roster of ten classical irezumi motifs to reveal the single best match. You'll get more than just a name: you'll learn the motif's traditional symbolism and a note on how it's classically composed or paired in real irezumi work, from Hokusai-style waves to companion flowers and wind bars. Explore the full Codex tab to browse all ten motifs and their meanings at your own pace, or dive straight into a personal reveal. Every reading is saved to your history so you can revisit past reflections. A limited number of free reveals are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad. Irezumi Codex is a guide to meaning and symbolism for inspiration and cultural appreciation. It does not generate tattoo designs or replace a conversation with a professional tattoo artist, who you should always consult before getting inked.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料診断回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された「大切にしたい想い・転機」のテキストは診断結果を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は診断リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

海外では鯉・龍・虎などの和彫りモチーフが「クールなデザイン」として広く知られる一方、その背景にある象徴的な意味や伝統的な構図の作法まで理解しているユーザーはごくわずかです。Irezumi Codexは、単なるデザイン集ではなく「自分の人生の物語」とモチーフを結びつけることで、和彫り文化への理解と敬意を深めながら楽しめる、これまでにないタイプの体験を提供します。
