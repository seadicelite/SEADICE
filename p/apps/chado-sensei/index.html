概要
The Japanese tea ceremony (chado, "the Way of Tea") has a rich, largely unwritten etiquette that can feel intimidating from the outside: what to wear, how to hold and turn the bowl, what to say to your host, how to behave at the low entrance you must crawl through, when to eat the sweet and when to drink. Most guides only offer static articles that don't address your specific situation. Chado Sensei lets you type in whatever you're actually facing — an upcoming invitation, something confusing that just happened at a tea gathering, a term you overheard and couldn't place — in your own words. Claude AI, playing the role of an experienced tea ceremony instructor, reads your exact situation and responds with clear, encouraging step-by-step guidance, names the single most relevant Japanese term for it, and flags the one mistake foreigners most often make in that exact moment. A separate Glossary tab covers twelve essential tea ceremony terms — from the bowl and whisk to the crawl-through entrance and the light meal served before formal tea — as a standalone reference you can browse any time, even without asking a question. Every question you ask is saved to your personal history, so you can look back on what you've learned. Whether you're preparing for your first formal tea gathering, traveling in Japan, or simply curious about a centuries-old practice, Chado Sensei turns "what am I supposed to do?" into a warm, specific answer.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告に加え、1日の無料質問回数（5回）を超えた場合はリワード広告の視聴で追加質問（+3回）が可能です。回答生成のためユーザーが入力した状況・疑問のテキストのみをAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信しますが、氏名・連絡先等を特定できる情報は送信しません。

今後実装予定の機能

  • 過去の質問をカテゴリー別に振り返れる分類機能
  • 回答結果をSNSでシェアする機能
  • フランス語・スペイン語など対応言語の追加

なぜこのアプリが必要か

茶道は日本人にとっても奥が深く、体系的に学ぶには時間がかかる文化ですが、海外の人が初めて触れる場合はなおさら、何が正しい作法なのか調べる手段自体が限られています。単なる用語解説にとどまらず、今まさに直面している状況に寄り添って答えるAIの先生がいることで、茶道への不安を安心に変え、文化への理解を深める体験を提供します。
