Promotional Text
Describe an occasion, a feeling, or someone you're designing for, and let AI create a Japanese folding fan design rooted in centuries-old traditional motifs — complete with a color palette, composition, and a phrase for the fan's edge.
Description
For centuries, Japanese artisans have painted sensu (folding fans) with a small, recurring set of motifs — cherry blossoms for fleeting beauty, cranes for longevity, waves for quiet resilience — each one carrying its own meaning and season. Sensu Muse brings this tradition to your pocket as a personal design studio. Simply describe an occasion, a feeling, or someone you're designing a fan for — a farewell gift, a wedding, a quiet autumn afternoon — and our AI, acting as a master fan artisan, selects the single best-matching motif from ten classical designs and writes a full concept: a color palette, how the composition would flow across the fan's folded panels, and a short original phrase suitable for calligraphy along its edge, all tied back to that motif's traditional symbolism. Every design is written in a warm, artisan's voice that draws on real Japanese fan-making history rather than generic decoration, giving you something that feels genuinely rooted in craft. A Motifs tab lets you browse all ten classical fan motifs and their meanings at any time, and your design history is saved so you can revisit past concepts. A limited number of free designs are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料デザイン回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力されたシーン・相手に関するテキストはデザイン案を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は生成リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

扇子は海外でも「日本らしいお土産」としての認知はあるものの、文様一つひとつの意味や職人の設計思想まで踏み込んで体験できる場はほとんどありません。Sensu Museは、誰もが持つ「贈りたい相手」「伝えたい気持ち」というパーソナルな情報を起点に、単なる工芸品紹介を超えた「自分だけの扇子デザイン」という体験に変えます。
