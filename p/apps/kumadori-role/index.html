Description
Kabuki actors don't just act a role — they wear it, painted directly onto their face in bold color-coded lines called kumadori. A hero's face blazes with crimson beni-guma. A villain's face is shadowed in cold indigo ai-guma. A monster wears earthy brown cha-guma. For four hundred years, Japanese audiences have read these patterns on sight to know exactly who they're watching. Kumadori Role brings that same reading to your real life. Describe a situation, feeling, or conflict you're facing right now — a hard conversation, a decision you're stalling on, a fight you can't shake — and our AI, speaking like a bold kabuki narrator, casts you in the single kumadori pattern that fits. You'll learn the pattern's name, its kanji, the kind of character who traditionally wears it, and one grounded, practical thought about how that character would handle exactly what you're going through. The Patterns tab is a full reference grid of all ten classical kumadori designs — beni-guma, ai-guma, cha-guma, mukimi-guma, ippon-guma, kuginuki-guma, sabi-guma, suji-guma, kani-guma, and namazu-guma — each with its kanji and meaning, so you can explore the tradition even without a reading. Your past readings are saved to a History tab so you can look back on every role you've played. A limited number of free readings are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料診断回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された状況・気持ちのテキストは診断結果を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は診断リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

「歌舞伎」は海外でも名前こそ知られていますが、役者の顔の色や線が役柄を語る記号だという奥深さまで知る人はほとんどいません。App Store内をライブ検索しても隈取をテーマにした競合アプリは1件も存在せず、手つかずのニッチです。Kumadori Roleは、この様式美を「自分の悩みを診断してもらう」というパーソナルな体験に変換することで、単なる文化紹介では終わらない、繰り返し使いたくなるアプリに仕上げています。
