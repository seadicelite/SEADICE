プロモーション用テキスト
Heard a word in Japan that sounded like English but didn't quite make sense? Type it in, and let AI decode what it really means, why, and how likely it is to confuse a native speaker.
概要
Wasei-eigo — literally "Japan-made English" — are words built from English-looking parts that carry a different meaning inside Japan. A "salaryman" isn't just an employee. A "consent" is a wall outlet, not permission. "Skinship" has nothing to do with romance. These words are everywhere in Japan, and they can trip up English speakers who assume they already know what they mean. Japanglish lets you type in any word or phrase you heard in Japan that sounded like English but didn't quite add up. Claude AI reads it and decodes what it actually means, explains why the meaning diverged from standard English, rates how likely a native English speaker is to misunderstand it if used as-is, and gives you a natural example sentence. Unlike a simple dictionary lookup, it's built to handle the exact word you actually heard — not just a fixed list. A separate Glossary tab covers 12 of the most common wasei-eigo terms as a standalone reference you can browse any time, even without decoding something new. Every word you decode is saved to your personal history so you can look back on what you've learned. Whether you live in Japan, are visiting, or are simply a language nerd who loves a good etymological rabbit hole, Japanglish turns a confusing moment into a small, satisfying "aha."

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告に加え、1日の無料解読回数（5回）を超えた場合はリワード広告の視聴で追加解読（+3回）が可能です。AI解読のためユーザーが入力した単語・フレーズのテキストのみをAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信しますが、氏名・連絡先等を特定できる情報は送信しません。

今後実装予定の機能

  • 用語集の単語をカテゴリ別（ビジネス・生活・ファッションなど）に絞り込む機能
  • 解読結果をSNSでシェアする機能
  • フランス語・スペイン語など対応言語の追加

なぜこのアプリが必要か

和製英語は日本語の中に深く溶け込んでいるため、日本人自身がそれを「本来の英語とは違う」と意識する機会は多くありません。一方で海外の英語話者にとっては、見た目が英単語そのものであるがゆえに、かえって誤解を招きやすい存在です。自分が実際に耳にした一語そのものについてAIが答えてくれることで、単なる用語集以上に「腑に落ちる」体験を提供します。
