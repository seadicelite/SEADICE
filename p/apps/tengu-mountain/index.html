Description
In Japanese folklore, tengu are mountain-dwelling spirits with a reputation for arrogance and mischief - but also for being ferociously skilled teachers of swordsmanship, archery, and self-discipline, provided the student is willing to be humbled first. Different real mountains across Japan are tied to specific tengu with specific personalities and specific lessons, and most of that detail never makes it past "red mask, long nose." Tengu Mountain turns your own situation into a way into that detail. Describe what you're struggling with or working toward - discipline, letting go of ego, patience, courage, starting over - and the AI matches you to a real tengu mountain: Kurama-yama in Kyoto, home of Sojobo, the tengu king said to have trained the young warrior Minamoto no Yoshitsune in swordsmanship; Hiko-san in Fukuoka, one of Japan's three great tengu mountains and a historic shugendo training ground for mountain ascetics; or Izuna-san in Nagano, associated with the tengu-like deity Izuna Gongen and a folk tradition of izuna-tsukai spirit mastery. Each match comes with the real legend behind that mountain's tengu, the specific discipline or virtue it's traditionally associated with, and an explanation of why that particular lesson fits what you described - written in the tengu's own stern-but-fair voice rather than as a generic motivational quote.

Why This App

Most casual explanations of tengu stop at the mask and the long nose, without covering which real mountain a given tengu belongs to or what specific discipline that tradition is actually about. Tengu Mountain is grounded in real mountains, real associated deities, and their documented folk traditions, turning a personal reflection you were already having into a structured way to learn the legend correctly.
