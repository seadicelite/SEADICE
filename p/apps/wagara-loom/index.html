Description
Long before "kawaii" or "kimono" became familiar words abroad, Japan was weaving meaning directly into fabric. Wagara — the country's traditional decorative patterns — are more than pretty geometry: asanoha's hexagonal leaves were sewn into baby clothes to wish for fast, healthy growth; seigaiha's rolling waves promised peaceful continuity; sayagata's interlocking key-fret lattice lined the finest kimono to symbolize an unbroken family line. Wagara Loom brings this centuries-old symbolism to your pocket. Simply describe what you're feeling or facing right now — starting a new job, missing someone, looking for a fresh start — and our AI reads your words against a curated roster of ten authentic wagara patterns, choosing the single best match and explaining its traditional meaning and where it has historically been worn or used, in a warm, personal reading. Browse the full Pattern Atlas at any time to explore all ten motifs — asanoha, seigaiha, shippou, kikko, uroko, ichimatsu, yagasuri, sayagata, kagome, and tatewaku — each with its kanji, name, and history. Every reading is saved to your personal history so you can look back on the patterns that have matched your journey. A limited number of free readings are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料診断回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された気分・状況のテキストは診断結果を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は診断リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

海外では着物や日本の伝統模様そのものへの関心は高い一方、個々の柄に込められた意味や由来まで踏み込んで紹介するコンテンツはまだ少ないのが現状です。Wagara Loomは、誰もが持つ「今の気分」というパーソナルな入り口から和柄の世界に触れてもらうことで、単なる図鑑的な知識ではなく「自分に語りかけてくる文様」として和柄を体験できる場所を作ります。
