Promotional Text
Make a vow the way Japan has for centuries: paint one eye of a daruma doll when you commit to a goal, and the second the day you achieve it. AI writes your vow.
Description
Daruma Vow brings a real Japanese tradition to your goals. A daruma is a round, hollow doll sold with two blank white eyes. The moment you commit to a goal, you paint in one eye and keep the doll where you will see it every day. Only when the goal is achieved do you paint the second eye and close the circle. Simply describe any goal you are working toward, and our AI generates a short, ceremonial vow in the spirit of this centuries-old ritual, one you can speak aloud while painting your daruma's first eye. Alongside your vow, you will receive three concrete milestones toward your goal and a steadying line of encouragement for the days it feels hard. Every vow you make is saved as a daruma in your personal collection. Watch over it with one eye until the goal is done, then mark it achieved to paint the second eye, complete with a nod to the real daruma-kuyo tradition of thanking and retiring a fulfilled doll. An About tab explains the eye-painting ritual in full, along with the real meanings behind six traditional daruma colors, from lucky red to scholarly blue. A limited number of free vows are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料誓い回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された目標テキストは誓い・マイルストーンを生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は生成リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

海外では新年の抱負やゴール設定アプリの需要は大きいものの、多くは無味乾燥なチェックリストに留まっています。Daruma Vowは、200年以上続く日本の「だるま」の目入れという物理的な儀式をデジタル上で再現することで、目標設定に文化的な重みと物語性を与え、単なるタスク管理を超えた継続のモチベーションを生み出します。
