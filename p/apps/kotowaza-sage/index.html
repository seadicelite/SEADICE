Description
Ever wished you had a wise elder to consult when facing a tough decision? For centuries, Japanese kotowaza - proverbs distilled from generations of lived experience - have offered exactly that: a single memorable saying that cuts straight to the heart of a situation. Kotowaza Sage lets you describe whatever you're facing - a hard choice, a worry, a moment of doubt - and Claude AI selects the single best-matching proverb from a roster of ten real, time-tested Japanese sayings, from "Even monkeys fall from trees" to "Fall seven times, rise eight." Each reading includes the original Japanese text, its romaji pronunciation, an English gloss, and a short, warm explanation connecting the proverb's traditional wisdom to your own situation. Explore the full Proverb Almanac to browse all ten sayings at your own pace, and revisit your past readings any time in your personal History. Five free readings are available every day; watching a short ad unlocks three more. Available in both English and Japanese.

Text you enter is sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate your reading, and is not stored permanently on our servers.

Why This App

Concepts like "ikigai" and "wabi-sabi" are already popular abroad, but Japan's rich tradition of kotowaza - proverbs passed down for centuries - remains largely undiscovered outside Japan, with only a handful of static dictionary apps attempting to catalog them. Kotowaza Sage turns a proverb from something you look up into something that speaks directly to whatever you're going through right now.
