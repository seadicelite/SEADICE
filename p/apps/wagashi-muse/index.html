概要
Wagashi are Japan's traditional confections — soft mochi, jellied bars, sculpted seasonal shapes — each one tied to a season, an occasion, or a particular cup of tea. But if you didn't grow up with them, telling a daifuku from a monaka, or knowing which one fits a quiet rainy afternoon versus a celebration, isn't obvious. Wagashi Muse lets you describe your mood, the season, an occasion, or the tea you're drinking, in your own words. Claude AI — playing the role of a wagashi shop's proprietor — reads your moment and recommends the single best-matching sweet from a roster of 10 classic wagashi, explaining why it fits, describing its texture and flavor, and suggesting which tea (matcha, sencha, hojicha, or genmaicha) pairs best with it. A separate Guide tab covers all 10 wagashi as a standalone reference you can browse any time, even without asking for a recommendation. Every recommendation you receive is saved to your personal history, so you can look back on the sweets that matched your past moments. Whether you're planning a tea time, shopping at a Japanese grocery store, or simply curious about a culture you love, Wagashi Muse turns "which one should I try?" into a warm, personal recommendation.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告に加え、1日の無料提案回数（5回）を超えた場合はリワード広告の視聴で追加提案（+3回）が可能です。提案機能のためユーザーが入力した気分・季節・お茶等のテキストのみをAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信しますが、氏名・連絡先等を特定できる情報は送信しません。

  • 提案結果をSNSでシェアする機能
  • フランス語・スペイン語など対応言語の追加

なぜこのアプリが必要か

和菓子は日本人にとって季節感と結びついた身近な存在ですが、海外の人にとっては種類が多く、どれが自分の気分や場面に合うのか判断しづらい文化です。単なる図鑑的な説明を超えて、自分が今感じている気分そのものに寄り添った一品をAIが提案することで、和菓子選びを「腑に落ちる」体験に変えます。
