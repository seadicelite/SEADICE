Promotional Text
Japan's old calendar divides the year into 72 microseasons of about five days each, each named for one small change in nature. See which one is unfolding today, write a moment from your life, and let AI reflect it back to you.
Description
Long before the seven-day week, Japan kept time with koyomi — an almanac that divided the solar year into 72 microseasons (shichijuu-ni kou), each lasting only a handful of days and named for one precise, small change in the natural world: "bush warblers start singing in the mountains," "first peach blossoms," "salmon gather upstream." Koyomi Seasons automatically figures out which of the 72 microseasons is unfolding right now, wherever you are, and shows it to you with its kanji, its romanized name, and the traditional meaning behind it. Then it invites you to write a small moment from your own day — a feeling, an observation, anything at all — and Claude, an AI writing in the quiet voice of an almanac-keeper, reflects that moment back to you through the imagery of the current season. It's never a prediction or advice, just a short, poetic noticing. An Almanac tab lets you browse all 72 microseasons at your own pace, with today's entry highlighted, so you can learn the full yearly cycle — a system rewritten specifically for Japan's climate in 1685 by the astronomer Shibukawa Shunkai, and almost unknown outside the country. A History tab keeps your past reflections as a quiet, ongoing journal. A limited number of free reflections are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料内省回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された一日の出来事のテキストは内省文を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は生成リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

海外では「ikigai」や「wabi-sabi」のような日本発の概念コンテンツはすでに人気がありますが、旧暦の細やかな季節区分「七十二候」はほぼ知られていません。二十四節気は中国由来として海外にもある程度知られている一方、七十二候は1685年に渋川春海が日本の気候に合わせて独自に書き改めたもので、正真正銘の日本固有の体系です。Koyomi Seasonsは、この知られざる暦を「今日の候」というパーソナルな形で毎日体験させることで、単なる読み物ではなく日々の小さな内省習慣に変えます。
