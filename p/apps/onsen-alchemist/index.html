Promotional Text
Describe how your body feels today, and let a traditional Japanese toji advisor match you to one of nine real onsen mineral spring types, with an AI-written reading and bathing tip.
Description
Japan's hot springs aren't just a soak — for centuries, each one has been classified by its dissolved minerals into distinct "senshitsu" types, each carrying its own therapeutic folklore passed down by toji, the traditional hot-spring cure advisors of Japanese ryokan. Onsen Alchemist brings that tradition into your pocket. Simply describe how your body and mind feel right now — sore shoulders, a lingering cold, low energy, stress — and our AI, acting as a veteran toji advisor, reads your condition and matches you to the single best-fitting spring type from nine real, officially classified Japanese onsen minerals: Simple, Chloride, Bicarbonate, Sulfate, Carbon-Dioxide, Iron, Sulfur, Acidic, and Radium springs. Each reading explains the traditional therapeutic properties behind your matched spring in a warm, authentic voice, and closes with a practical bathing tip such as ideal soak duration or time of day. Every reading is saved to your personal history so you can look back on how your matches have changed over time. The Spring Codex tab is a standalone reference guide to all nine spring types, their kanji names, and the real folklore behind each one — genuine Japanese cultural content you can explore anytime, AI reading or not. A limited number of free readings are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料診断回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力された体調テキストは診断結果を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は診断リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

海外では「onsen」という言葉自体はすでに知られていても、日本の温泉法が定める9種類の泉質やその効能の言い伝えまで踏み込んだコンテンツはほとんど流通していません。Onsen Alchemistは、旅行ガイド的な温泉紹介ではなく、「今日の自分の体調」というパーソナルな入力を起点に泉質の世界を体験させることで、単なる読み物を超えた「自分ごと」の湯治体験に変えます。
