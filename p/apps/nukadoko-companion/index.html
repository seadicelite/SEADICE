Promotional Text
Keep your Japanese nukadoko (rice-bran pickle bed) thriving. Answer a few quick questions about its smell, surface, and mixing habits, and let AI diagnose its health, suggest care steps, and recommend what to pickle next.
Description
Nukadoko, the living rice-bran pickle bed at the heart of Japanese tsukemono culture, has been passed down through generations of households — and it can be finicky. A healthy bed needs daily mixing and careful reading of its smell and surface to stay balanced, and it's easy for beginners to feel lost. Nukadoko Companion turns that expertise into a simple AI checklist. Tell the app what your bed smells like, how its surface looks, how often you've been mixing it, and how long it's been since you last touched it, plus an optional note about anything unusual. Our AI, grounded in real nukazuke fermentation science, reads these signals the way an experienced nukadoko keeper would and returns a clear diagnosis of your bed's current health, concrete care instructions to fix or maintain it, and a specific vegetable recommendation for what to pickle in it next. A companion Encyclopedia tab introduces twelve real tsukemono varieties — from tangy umeboshi to deep, alcohol-scented narazuke — each with its own short history and how it's traditionally made, so you can explore the wider world of Japanese pickling culture beyond your own nukadoko. Your diagnosis history is saved so you can track your bed's health over time. A limited number of free diagnoses are available each day, with the option to unlock more by watching a short ad.

広告について

本アプリはGoogle AdMobによる広告を表示します。画面下部のバナー広告のほか、1日の無料診断回数には上限（5回）があり、上限到達後はリワード広告視聴で追加3回分を解放できます。入力されたぬか床の状態（におい・表面・かき混ぜ頻度・補足メモ）は診断結果を生成するためAnthropic社のClaude APIに送信されますが、送信は診断リクエストの処理のためのみに利用され、当社サーバーに永続的に保存されることはありません。画像や位置情報は一切送信されません。

なぜこのアプリが必要か

海外では発酵食への関心が高まっているものの、日本の「ぬか床」のように毎日の手入れと経験に基づく判断が必要な文化を正しくサポートするアプリはまだ少ないのが現状です。Nukadoko Companionは、経験豊富な作り手が無意識に行っている「におい・表面・頻度から状態を読み取る」プロセスをAIで再現することで、初心者でも安心してぬか床を続けられる体験を提供します。
