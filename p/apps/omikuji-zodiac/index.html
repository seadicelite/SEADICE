Description
Ever wondered what it's like to draw an omikuji at a real Japanese shrine? Omikuji Zodiac brings this centuries-old tradition to your pocket, blended with the twelve zodiac animals that have guided Japanese fortune-telling for generations. Simply enter your birth year, and the app instantly reveals your zodiac animal along with its traditional kanji character. Our AI then generates a fortune in the authentic style of a shrine omikuji: a blessing grade (from Great Blessing to Curse) followed by specific guidance on love, work, health, and general advice. Each reading is written in a solemn, poetic tone that echoes the real paper slips handed out at shrines across Japan, giving you an experience that feels genuinely rooted in Japanese culture rather than a generic horoscope. Your fortune history is saved so you can look back on past readings, and the Zodiac tab lets you explore all twelve animals and their kanji characters. Just like a real shrine visit, one reading is available per day.

About Ads

This app displays ads via Google AdMob, including a banner ad at the bottom of the screen. Just like an actual shrine visit, only one free fortune is available per day (there is no way to unlock additional draws). The zodiac sign calculated from your birth year is sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate your reading, and is not permanently stored on our servers.

Why This App

Concepts like "ikigai" and "wabi-sabi" are already popular abroad, but few interactive apps do them justice. Omikuji Zodiac combines the omikuji - a symbol of visiting a Japanese shrine - with something everyone has, their birth year, turning it from something you just read about into something personal.
